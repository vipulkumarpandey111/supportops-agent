# Cloudnest Billing Policy

## Refund Eligibility
Cloudnest offers a full refund within 14 days of any charge if the customer
has used less than 5GB of storage during that billing cycle. Refund requests
after 14 days are only granted in cases of a proven billing error (e.g. a
duplicate charge or a charge after cancellation).

## Duplicate Charges
If a customer was charged twice for the same billing cycle due to a payment
processor error, the duplicate charge is refunded automatically within 3
business days. No approval is required for confirmed duplicate charges.

## Subscription Plans
Cloudnest has three plans: Starter ($5/mo, 100GB), Pro ($15/mo, 1TB), and
Business ($40/mo, 5TB, includes team sharing). Downgrading a plan takes
effect at the next billing cycle; upgrading is immediate and prorated.

## Cancellations
Customers can cancel anytime from Account Settings. Cancellation stops
future billing but does not refund the current billing cycle unless the
Refund Eligibility criteria above are met.

## Refund Approval Thresholds
Refunds under $50 can be auto-approved by the support agent. Refunds of
$50 or more require escalation to a human billing specialist before being
issued.
